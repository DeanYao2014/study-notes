### 1.2 语法核心：不定代词 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> 不定代词是初中英语**选择/完形填空高频考点**，核心是区分"两者"和"三者及以上"。

#### 核心五词一览

| 代词 | 含义 | 指代范围 | 谓语 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| **both** | 两者都 | 两个人/物 | 复数 | **Both** of them are students. |
| **all** | 全都 | 三者及以上 | 单/复 | **All** of the students **are** here. |
| **either** | 两者任一 | 两者中选一 | 单数 | **Either** is OK. / You can take **either** side. |
| **neither** | 两者都不 | 两者全否定 | 单数 | **Neither** of the answers **is** correct. |
| **none** | 全无 | 三者及以上 | 单/复 | **None** of the food **was** eaten. |

#### 关键句型

| 句型 | 结构 | 考点 |
|------|------|------|
| both...and... | Both Tom **and** Jack **are** tall. | 谓语用**复数** |
| either...or... | **Either** you **or** he **is** wrong. | **就近原则** |
| neither...nor... | **Neither** she **nor** I **am** right. | **就近原则** |
| not only...but also... | Not only he **but also** they **are** coming. | **就近原则** |

#### 辨析：none vs no one

| | none | no one |
|------|------|------|
| 指**人/物** | ✅ | ❌ 只指人 |
| 可跟 **of** | ✅ none of them | ❌ no one of them |
| 谓语 | 单/复均可 | 只单数 |

## 四、课文重点句子讲解

> 以下句子来自 Unit 2 两篇主课文，每句拆解：语法点 + 仿写。

### 4.1 课文 A：马拉松

#### 句 1

> 🇬🇧 **All of them held their breath. Would he make it?**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **All** of them | all = 全都（指所有观众，三者以上） |
| **held** their breath | hold → held（不规则过去式）；hold one's breath = 屏住呼吸 |
| Would he make it? | would = 会不会（推测）；make it = 成功完成 |

> 📝 **仿写**：All of us held our breath. Would she win the first prize?

---

#### 句 2（含不定代词 both）

> 🇬🇧 **Both runners were tired, but neither of them wanted to give up.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **Both** runners | both + 复数名词 = 两个都 |
| **neither** of them | neither + of + 代词 = 两者都不 |
| wanted to give up | give up = 放弃（give → gave → given） |
| but 转折 | 两者都累，但谁也不放弃 → 对比 |

> 📝 **仿写**：Both teams were exhausted, but neither of them stopped fighting.

---

#### 句 3

> 🇬🇧 **However, the players never gave up. They took every chance to learn and improve.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| However | 然而（注意逗号！） |
| never **gave** up | give → gave（不规则过去式） |
| took every chance | take → took；take a chance = 抓住机会 |
| to learn and improve | 不定式表目的 = 为了学习和提高 |

> 📝 **仿写**：However, the students never gave up. They took every chance to practise and grow.

---

#### 句 4（含不定代词 none）

> 🇬🇧 **There were five teams in the competition, but none of them could break the school record.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| There were five teams | there be 句型，一般过去时 |
| **none** of them | none = 全无（五队中一个都没有） |
| could break | could + 动词原形 = 能够 |
| school record | 校记录 |

> 📝 **仿写**：There were ten students in the race, but none of them could finish in under three minutes.

---

### 4.2 课文 B：体育精神

#### 句 5

> 🇬🇧 **There are still many difficulties ahead. But they will rise to every challenge, thanks to their shining spirit.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| difficulties ahead | ahead = 在前方（后置定语修饰 difficulties） |
| rise to every challenge | rise to = 应对/迎战（固定搭配） |
| **thanks to** | 由于；多亏了（= because of） |
| shining spirit | 闪亮的精神（shining = 发光的，比喻用法） |

> 📝 **仿写**：There are still many problems ahead. But we will rise to every challenge, thanks to our strong teamwork.

---

#### 句 6

> 🇬🇧 **It doesn't matter whether you win or lose. What matters is that you tried your best.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| It doesn't matter | 没关系（matter = 重要/要紧） |
| whether...or... | 无论是……还是…… |
| **What matters** | What 引导的名词性从句作主语 = 重要的是…… |
| tried your best | try one's best = 尽力 |

> 📝 **仿写**：It doesn't matter whether you succeed or fail. What matters is that you learned something.

---

#### 句 7（含不定代词 either）

> 🇬🇧 **You can choose either path — the easy one or the hard one. The hard one will make you stronger.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **either** path | either + 单数名词 = 两者中任一 |
| the easy one or the hard one | 破折号引出解释 |
| will make you stronger | make + 宾语 + 比较级 = 使……更…… |

> 📝 **仿写**：You can take either route — the short one or the scenic one. The scenic one will give you better memories.

---

#### 句 8

> 🇬🇧 **A true champion is not the one who always wins, but the one who never stops trying.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| not...but... | 不是……而是…… |
| the one who... | 那个……的人（who 引导定语从句修饰 the one） |
| always wins | 总是赢（一般现在时表常态） |
| never stops trying | 从不停止尝试（stop doing） |

> 📝 **仿写**：A true leader is not the one who gives orders, but the one who sets an example.

## 五、易错点清单

| # | 易错点 | 正解 | 错例分析 |
|---|--------|------|------|
| 1 | both 指两者 vs all 指三者+ | **Both** my parents are teachers. / **All** my classmates like sports. | ❌ Both of my three friends...（两者不能用 three） |
| 2 | either...or... **就近原则** | Either you **or** he **is** wrong. | ❌ Either you or he are wrong. |
| 3 | neither...nor... **就近原则** | Neither she **nor** I **am** going. | ❌ Neither she nor I are going. |
| 4 | none 可跟 of / no one 不跟 of | **None** of them came. / **No one** came. | ❌ No one of them came. |
| 5 | neither 主语 → 谓语**单数** | Neither of the books **is** mine. | ❌ Neither of the books are mine. |
| 6 | raise（及物）vs rise（不及物） | **raise** money / the sun **rises** | ❌ The sun raises. |
| 7 | lead → **led**（不是 lead） | He **led** the team to victory. | ❌ He leaded the team. |
| 8 | proud（of 搭配） | be proud **of** | ❌ be proud for |

#### Passage A

The marathon is one of the most challenging（有挑战性的）races in the world — 42.195 kilometres of non-stop running. But did you know how it got its name?

The story goes back to 490 BC（公元前）, in ancient Greece（古希腊）. The Greeks had just won a great battle（战役）against their enemies at a place called Marathon. A soldier named Pheidippides was chosen to run from Marathon to Athens（雅典）— about 40 kilometres — to tell the good news.

Pheidippides ran the whole distance without stopping. When he arrived in Athens, he shouted, "We have won!" and then fell down and died from exhaustion（力竭）.

The modern marathon was born at the first modern Olympic Games in 1896, held in Athens. The race was 40 kilometres long — about the same as Pheidippides's famous run. Later, the distance was changed to 42.195 kilometres, which is today's official marathon distance.

Today, people run marathons not just to win, but to challenge themselves. Many runners raise money for charity（慈善）. For most people, finishing a marathon is a great personal achievement — no matter what place they come in.

### 七、书面表达（参考范文）

My Sports Story

Last year, I joined the school's 1,500-metre race. I was nervous because I was not a fast runner.

During the race, I fell behind quickly. After the first lap（圈）, I wanted to give up. But my classmates shouted, "Go for it! You can do it!" Their cheers gave me courage. I kept running, step by step.

I finished last, but I was proud of myself. I learned that winning is not everything — what matters is that you never give up. The spirit of trying your best is the real victory.
