### 1.2 语法核心：一般过去时 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> 这是七年级下册**最重要的语法点**，几乎所有单元测试、期中/期末都会重点考察！

#### be 动词过去式

| 人称 | 现在 | 过去 |
|------|------|------|
| I | am | **was** |
| he/she/it | is | **was** |
| we/you/they | are | **were** |

#### 实义动词过去式

```
肯定：主语 + 动词过去式
否定：主语 + didn't + 动词原形
疑问：Did + 主语 + 动词原形？
```

**时间标志词**：yesterday, last week/month/year, ...ago, in 2008, just now, the other day, at that moment

#### 规则动词过去式拼写

| 规则 | 例词 | 过去式 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般 +ed | walk, play | walked, played |
| 以 e 结尾 +d | like, live | liked, lived |
| 辅音+y → ied | study, carry | studied, carried |
| 重读闭音节双写+ed | stop, plan | stopped, planned |

#### 本单元高频不规则动词（必背！）

| 原形 | 过去式 | 原形 | 过去式 |
|------|--------|------|--------|
| put | **put** | keep | **kept** |
| sweep | **swept** | come | **came** |
| blow | **blew** | fall | **fell** |
| forget | **forgot** | choose | **chose** |
| feel | **felt** | give | **gave** |
| make | **made** | tell | **told** |
| find | **found** | think | **thought** |

## 四、课文重点句子讲解

> 以下句子来自 Unit 1 两篇主课文，每句拆解：语法点 + 仿写。

### 4.1 课文 A：一个关于幸福的故事

#### 句 1

> 🇬🇧 **Poor in things, rich in love.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| Poor in things | 物质上贫穷（poor + in + 方面） |
| rich in love | 在爱上富有（rich + in + 方面） |
| 省略句 | 省略了主语和 be 动词，常用于格言/对比 |
| 对偶修辞 | poor ↔ rich，things ↔ love |

> 📝 **仿写**：Poor in money, rich in friends.

---

#### 句 2

> 🇬🇧 **To my surprise, the family were happy together.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **To my surprise** | 令我惊讶的是（to one's + 名词 = 令某人……的是） |
| the family **were** | family 此处指家庭成员，谓语用复数 |
| happy together | 在一起很幸福（together 强调团聚） |

> 📝 **仿写**：To my surprise, the old man lived alone but never felt lonely.

---

#### 句 3（含过去时）

> 🇬🇧 **I once visited a poor family. What I saw there changed my understanding of happiness.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **once** visited | once = 曾经，和时间标志词一样，句子用过去时 |
| **What I saw** | What 引导的名词性从句 = 我所看到的（东西） |
| **changed** | change 的过去式 = 改变了 |
| understanding of... | 对……的理解（of 介词） |

> 📝 **仿写**：I once met an old teacher. What she told me changed my view of learning.

---

#### 句 4

> 🇬🇧 **Keep love in your heart. A life without it is like a sunless garden.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **Keep** love | 祈使句，Keep + 宾语 + 介词短语 = 把……保持在…… |
| without it | 没有它（it = love） |
| is **like**... | like 此处是介词 = 像……一样 |
| sunless garden | sun + less = 没有阳光的（-less = 无） |

> 📝 **仿写**：Keep hope in your heart. A life without it is like a starless night.

---

### 4.2 课文 B：幸福源于何处

#### 句 5

> 🇬🇧 **Happiness springs from doing good and helping others. — Plato**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| springs from | spring from = 源于（不是"春天"！） |
| **doing** good | 做好事（good 此处是名词 = 好事） |
| helping others | 帮助他人（others = 其他人） |
| 介词 from + doing | from 后用动名词 |

> 📝 **仿写**：Confidence springs from practising and never giving up.

---

#### 句 6（含过去时）

> 🇬🇧 **He chose to stay and help, though it was freezing cold outside.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **chose** | choose 的过去式（不规则！） |
| chose **to** stay | choose to do = 选择做某事 |
| though = although | 虽然（引导让步状语从句） |
| **freezing** cold | freezing = 极冷的（修饰 cold，程度副词功能） |

> 📝 **仿写**：She decided to go out, though it was raining heavily.

---

#### 句 7

> 🇬🇧 **The wind blew hard all night long, but the little house was full of warmth and laughter.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **blew** | blow 的过去式（不规则！） |
| hard | 副词 = 猛烈地（修饰 blew） |
| all night long | 整夜（时间状语） |
| was **full of** | be full of = 充满…… |
| warmth | warm 的名词 = 温暖 |

> 📝 **仿写**：The rain fell heavily all day long, but the classroom was full of energy and excitement.

---

#### 句 8

> 🇬🇧 **I thought happiness meant having a lot of things. In fact, I was wrong.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **thought** | think 的过去式（不规则！） |
| meant | mean 的过去式 = 意味着 |
| having a lot of things | 动名词短语作宾语 |
| **In fact** | 事实上（转折，引出真相） |
| **was** wrong | was = 过去式（我过去错了，现在对了） |

> 📝 **仿写**：I thought success meant getting high scores. In fact, I was wrong. It means learning and growing.

## 五、易错点清单

| # | 易错点 | 正解 | 错例分析 |
|---|--------|------|------|
| 1 | 否定句/疑问句**动词还原** | Did you **go** there? | ❌ Did you went there? |
| 2 | didn't + **动词原形** | He didn't **come**. | ❌ He didn't came. |
| 3 | 不规则动词变化记错 | choose → **chose** | ❌ choose → choosed |
| 4 | 过去式与现在式混用 | I **went** there yesterday. | ❌ I go there yesterday. |
| 5 | was/were 混用 | I/He/She **was** / We/You/They **were** | ❌ I were / They was |
| 6 | exciting（物）vs excited（人） | The film is **exciting**. I'm **excited**. | 混用 |
| 7 | to one's surprise 中 one's 变形 | to **my** surprise | ❌ to I surprise |
| 8 | keep **doing** / decide **to do** | keep **trying** / decide **to go** | ❌ keep to try / decide going |

#### Passage A

Once there was a king who had everything — gold, palaces, and servants. But he was not happy. He always felt that something was missing（缺失的）.

One day, the king heard about a poor farmer who was said to be the happiest man in the country. The king decided to visit him. He put on simple clothes and went to the farmer's house.

The farmer lived in a small hut（小屋）with his wife and three children. They had almost nothing, but everyone was smiling. The farmer welcomed the king and offered him some simple food.

"How can you be so happy when you have so little?" the king asked.

The farmer smiled and said, "Your Majesty（陛下）, happiness is not about how much you have. It's about how much you enjoy what you have. I have a loving family, good health, and work that I enjoy. To me, that is true wealth（财富）."

The king thought about this for a long time. Finally, he understood. He returned to his palace not as a king looking for happiness, but as a man who finally found it — in a poor farmer's words.

Last summer, I volunteered（做志愿者）at a children's home. I went there every Saturday to help take care of the kids. At first, I just wanted to get some volunteer experience. But what happened there changed my life.

### 七、书面表达（参考范文）

A Happy Little Thing

Last Saturday, I helped an old lady carry her heavy bags to her home. She lived on the fifth floor, and there was no lift in the building. When we finally reached her door, she smiled and said, "Thank you, young man. You made my day."

Those simple words filled my heart with warmth. I felt so happy, not because I did something big, but because I made someone else's life a little easier.

I think happiness springs from small acts of kindness. You don't need to be rich to be happy — you just need a heart that is willing to give.
