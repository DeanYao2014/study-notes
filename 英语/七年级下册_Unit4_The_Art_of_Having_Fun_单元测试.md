### 1.2 语法核心：祈使句 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> 祈使句是英语中**最直接、最实用的句型之一**，用于发出指令、建议、请求或警告。

#### 四种祈使句类型

| 类型 | 肯定式 | 否定式 | 用途 |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| **Do 型** | **动词原形** + 其他 | **Don't** + 动词原形 + 其他 | 动作指令 |
| **Be 型** | **Be** + 形容词/名词 | **Don't be** + 形容词/名词 | 状态/品质 |
| **Let 型** | **Let** + 宾语 + 动词原形 | **Don't let**... / Let...**not**... | 建议/允许 |
| **No 型** | — | **No** + 动名词/名词 | 禁止（标牌用语） |

#### 例句对照

| 类型 | 肯定 | 否定 |
|------|------|------|
| Do 型 | **Take** a break. (休息一下) | **Don't sit** on the couch all day. |
| Be 型 | **Be** active! (要积极!) | **Don't be** lazy. |
| Let 型 | **Let's go** outside! | **Don't let** him stay up late. |
| No 型 | — | **No smoking.** / **No running.** |

#### 祈使句 + and/or（中考高频）

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 祈使句 + **and** | ……，那么（就会）…… | Take a break **and** you'll feel better. |
| 祈使句 + **or** | ……，否则…… | Hurry up, **or** you'll be late. |

> 🔥 口诀：and 顺接（好结果），or 逆接（坏结果）

## 四、课文重点句子讲解 🆕

> 以下句子来自 Unit 4 两篇主课文，每句拆解：语法点 + 仿写。

### 4.1 课文 A：The Importance of Having Fun

#### 句 1

> 🇬🇧 **All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| All work and no play | 主语是一个整体概念：只有工作没有玩耍 |
| **makes** | 谓语用**单数**（整体概念作主语，谓语单数） |
| Jack a dull boy | make + 宾语 + 宾补 = 使杰克变成无趣的孩子 |

> 📝 **仿写**：All study and no rest makes students tired and bored.

---

#### 句 2

> 🇬🇧 **Fun can do away with bad feelings and lift our spirits.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| can do away with | 情态动词 can + 动词短语 do away with = 能消除 |
| bad feelings | 坏情绪（feeling 在此 = 情绪） |
| and lift our spirits | lift one's spirits = 提振精神（spirit 用复数 spirits） |
| 平行结构 | can (do away with...) and (lift...) — 一个 can 管两个动词 |

> 📝 **仿写**：Sports can do away with stress and lift our energy levels.

---

#### 句 3

> 🇬🇧 **Take time out from your busy life and do something you enjoy.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **Take** time out | 祈使句，动词原形开头 |
| from your busy life | 从你忙碌的生活中 |
| **and** + do something | 祈使句 + and = ……那么……（顺承好结果） |
| you enjoy | 定语从句省略 that/which，修饰 something |

> 📝 **仿写**：Take a short break from your studies and do something that makes you happy.

---

#### 句 4

> 🇬🇧 **Don't spend all your free time on screens. Go outside and explore!**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **Don't spend** | 祈使句否定 = Don't + 动词原形 |
| spend...on... | 在……上花（时间/钱） |
| **Go** outside | 第二个祈使句，动词原形开头 |
| and explore | 祈使句 + and = ……去探索吧 |

> 📝 **仿写**：Don't stay at home all day. Go out and play with your friends!

---

### 4.2 课文 B：Ways to Have Fun

#### 句 5

> 🇬🇧 **We don't stop playing because we grow old; we grow old because we stop playing.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| don't stop playing | 不要停止玩耍（stop doing = 停止做某事） |
| because we grow old | because 引导原因状语从句 |
| **分号 ;** | 连接两个并列句，形成对称对比 |
| 名言出处 | 萧伯纳（George Bernard Shaw） |

> 📝 **仿写**：We don't stop learning because we grow old; we grow old because we stop learning.

---

#### 句 6

> 🇬🇧 **It's important to have a balanced life — both work and play matter.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **It's important to** do | 做……是重要的（It 是形式主语，to do 是真正主语） |
| a **balanced** life | 平衡的生活（balanced 是形容词，修饰 life） |
| **破折号 —** | 解释说明 |
| both...and... | 两者都（谓语用复数 matter） |

> 📝 **仿写**：It's important to eat healthily — both vegetables and meat matter.

---

#### 句 7（含祈使句）

> 🇬🇧 **Be open-minded and try new things. You might discover a new hobby!**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **Be** open-minded | Be 型祈使句：Be + 形容词 |
| **try** new things | Do 型祈使句：动词原形 + 宾语 |
| might discover | might = 可能（比 may 更不确定） |
| hobby | 爱好（复数为 hobbies） |

> 📝 **仿写**：Be brave and try the new food. You might discover something delicious!

---

#### 句 8

> 🇬🇧 **No one is an island. We all need to connect with others and have fun together.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| No one is an island | 名言：没有人是一座孤岛（John Donne） |
| need to connect | need to do = 需要做某事 |
| with others | 与他人（others = 其他人） |
| have fun together | 一起开心（have fun = 玩得开心） |

> 📝 **仿写**：No one is an island. We all need friends and should care about each other.

## 五、易错点清单

| # | 易错点 | 正解 | 错例分析 |
|---|--------|------|------|
| 1 | 祈使句否定用 **Don't** | **Don't be** late. | ❌ Not be late. / Be not late. |
| 2 | Don't 后永远**动词原形** | Don't **go** there. | ❌ Don't goes there. |
| 3 | bored（人）vs boring（物） | I'm **bored**. / The film is **boring**. | ❌ I'm boring.（变成"我是个无趣的人"） |
| 4 | alone（独自）vs lonely（孤独） | He lives **alone** but never feels **lonely**. | 混用 |
| 5 | stop **doing**（停止做）vs stop **to do**（停下来去做） | stop **playing** = 停止玩耍 | ❌ stop to play（停下正在做的事,去玩） |
| 6 | make + 宾语 + **形容词** | make Jack **dull** | ❌ make Jack dully |
| 7 | spend...**on** sth. / spend...**(in) doing** | spend time **on** screens | ❌ spend time to watch screens |
| 8 | **fun**（不可数）/ **funny**（好笑的） | have **fun** / a **funny** joke | ❌ have a funny（完全错） |

#### Passage A

Do you often feel tired and bored after school? You are not alone. Many students spend hours on homework and have little time for fun. But experts（专家）say that having fun is just as important as studying.

First, fun activities can do away with bad feelings. When you are doing something you enjoy — like playing sports, reading a novel, or listening to music — your brain（大脑）releases "happy chemicals" that lift your spirits.

Second, having fun with friends helps you build social skills（社交技能）. Playing team sports, for example, teaches you how to work with others and solve problems together.

Third, taking breaks actually helps you study better. Your brain needs rest to process（处理）information. If you study for too long without a break, you will feel tired and learn less.

So, what should you do? Here are some tips:
- **Take a short break** after every 45 minutes of study.
- **Spend at least one hour a day** on a hobby you love.
- **Don't use screens during your break** — go for a walk or talk to your family instead.
- **Try something new** every week — it could be a new sport, a new book, or a new skill.

Remember the old saying: "All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy." Be active, stay curious, and have fun!

### 七、书面表达（参考范文）

Dear Wang Tao,

I heard that you've been working really hard these days. I understand the pressure, but remember: all work and no play makes Jack a dull boy. It's important to have a balanced life.

Here are my suggestions. First, **take a short break** every hour — just stand up and stretch. Second, **do some sports** after school. Go jogging or play tennis, and you will feel much fresher. Third, **don't use screens before sleep** — read a novel or listen to music instead.

Take time out and enjoy your life. I'm sure you will study better when you feel happier!

Yours,
Li Hua
