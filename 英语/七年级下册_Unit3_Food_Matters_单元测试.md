### 1.2 语法核心：连系动词 ⭐⭐⭐

> 连系动词的**唯一考点**：后面接**形容词**，不接副词！

#### 四类连系动词

| 类别 | 动词 | 例句 | 考点 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **be 动词** | am, is, are, was, were | The food **is** delicious. | 最基础 |
| **感官类** | taste, smell, feel, sound, look | It **tastes** good. / It **smells** nice. | 后接**形容词**！ |
| **变化类** | become, get, turn, go, grow | The meat **went** bad. / She **became** angry. | go bad = 变质 |
| **保持类** | keep, remain, stay | **Keep** calm. / **Stay** healthy. | 保持某种状态 |

#### 高频句型

| 句型 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| S + 感官动词 + adj. | The soup **tastes salty**. | ❌ tastes saltily |
| S + 变化动词 + adj. | The food **went bad**. | go + bad/wrong/mad |
| S + 保持动词 + adj. | Please **keep quiet**. | stay + calm/healthy/safe |
| S + be + adj. | The rice **is delicious**. | 最基础的系表结构 |

> 🔥 **终极考点**：连系动词 = 形容词（表语），永远不用副词！
> - ✅ It tastes **good**. / It smells **wonderful**. / It feels **soft**.
> - ❌ It tastes **well**. / It smells **wonderfully**. / It feels **softly**.

## 四、课文重点句子讲解

> 以下句子来自 Unit 3 两篇主课文，每句拆解：语法点 + 仿写。

### 4.1 课文 A：食物的记忆

#### 句 1（含连系动词 taste/smell）

> 🇬🇧 **The taste and smell of a certain food can often bring back memories.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| The taste and smell | 两个名词并列作主语，谓语用复数（can） |
| a certain food | certain = 某种（不具体指明是哪一种） |
| can...bring back | can + 动词原形 = 能…… |
| bring back memories | bring back = 带回（唤起）；memories = 回忆（常用复数） |

> 📝 **仿写**：The sound and smell of rain can often bring back memories of my childhood.

---

#### 句 2（含连系动词）

> 🇬🇧 **The soup tasted delicious, but it looked very plain.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **tasted** delicious | taste + 形容词（⚠ 不是 deliciously） |
| **looked** plain | look + 形容词 = 看起来清淡 |
| but 转折 | 味道好但看起来普通——外表 vs 内在 |

> 📝 **仿写**：The cake looked simple, but it tasted amazing.

---

#### 句 3

> 🇬🇧 **The porridge smelt so good that I couldn't wait to try it.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **smelt** good | smell + 形容词（过去式 smelt/smelled） |
| so...that... | 如此……以至于……（结果状语从句） |
| couldn't wait to try | could 的否定 + wait to do = 等不及要做 |

> 📝 **仿写**：The barbecue smelt so good that everyone started to line up.

---

#### 句 4

> 🇬🇧 **Food is not just about eating. It brings people together and creates special moments.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| not just about... | 不仅仅是关于…… |
| brings...together | bring sb. together = 让人们聚在一起 |
| creates special moments | 创造特别时刻（moments = 时刻，瞬间） |
| 平行结构 | brings + creates — 两个谓语并列 |

> 📝 **仿写**：Music is not just about listening. It connects people and creates unforgettable memories.

---

### 4.2 课文 B：食物是文化的桥梁

#### 句 5

> 🇬🇧 **Many people see ice cream as a Western dessert, but its history is more complicated.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **see...as...** | 把……视为……（= regard...as...） |
| a Western dessert | Western = 西方的；dessert = 甜点 |
| but 转折 | 人们以为冰淇淋是西方的，但历史更复杂 |
| complicated | 复杂的（⚠ 不是 complexed） |

> 📝 **仿写**：Many people see pizza as Italian food, but its history is more interesting than you think.

---

#### 句 6（含连系动词）

> 🇬🇧 **Roujiamo tastes similar to a hamburger, but its history goes back much further.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **tastes** similar to | taste + 形容词（similar to 整体作表语） |
| similar **to** | similar 后接 to，不是 with |
| goes back | 追溯（go back = 追溯到） |
| much further | much + 比较级 = ……得多 |

> 📝 **仿写**：Jiaozi tastes similar to ravioli, but its history goes back much further in China.

---

#### 句 7

> 🇬🇧 **People often compare roujiamo to hamburgers, a symbol of American food.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **compare...to...** | 把……比作……（⚠ compare to 是比喻，compare with 是对比差异） |
| a symbol of... | ……的象征（同位语，补充说明 hamburgers） |
| American food | American = 美国的（⚠ American 不是 America） |

> 📝 **仿写**：People often compare the Yangtze River to the Nile, a symbol of Egypt.

---

#### 句 8

> 🇬🇧 **Food is a bridge between cultures. When we share a meal, we share more than just food.**

| 拆解 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| a bridge between cultures | 文化之间的桥梁（⚠ between 后 cultures 用复数） |
| When we share a meal | when 引导时间状语从句 |
| more than just... | 不仅仅是…… |
| 递进 | 分享食物 → 分享故事/情感/文化 |

> 📝 **仿写**：Music is a bridge between generations. When we sing together, we share more than just songs.

## 五、易错点清单

| # | 易错点 | 正解 | 错例分析 |
|---|--------|------|------|
| 1 | 连系动词 + **形容词** | tastes **delicious** | ❌ tastes deliciously |
| 2 | smell → **smelt**/smelled | The soup **smelt** wonderful. | 不规则过去式记错 |
| 3 | compare **to**（比作）vs **with**（比较） | compare life **to** a journey | ❌ compare life with a journey（变成比较异同了） |
| 4 | see...**as**... | see ice cream **as** a dessert | ❌ see ice cream to a dessert |
| 5 | go **bad**（变质） | The milk went **bad**. | ❌ The milk went badly.（变成"很糟糕地去"） |
| 6 | similar **to** | be similar **to** | ❌ be similar with |
| 7 | taste（尝起来）vs tasty（美味的） | The soup **tastes** good. / The soup is **tasty**. | 混淆系动词和形容词 |
| 8 | 感官动词没有进行时 | It **tastes** good. | ❌ It is tasting good.（除非表"正在品尝"这个动作） |

#### Passage A

Every country has its own "comfort food" — food that makes people feel warm and happy. In the UK, many people think of fish and chips. In Japan, it might be a hot bowl of ramen（拉面）. In Mexico, it could be tacos（墨西哥卷饼）. But what makes a food "comfort food"?

Scientists say comfort food is usually connected to happy memories from childhood. The smell of freshly baked bread may remind you of weekends at your grandmother's house. The taste of chicken soup may bring back memories of your mother taking care of you when you were sick.

Comfort food is also often simple and easy to make. It is usually high in carbohydrates（碳水化合物）and fat, which can actually make your brain release chemicals that lift your mood（提升情绪）. That's why a bowl of hot noodles or a piece of chocolate cake can make you feel better when you're feeling down.

However, nutritionists（营养学家）say we should not depend too much on comfort food. While it's OK to enjoy it sometimes, a balanced diet（均衡饮食）with enough vegetables, fruit, and protein（蛋白质）is still the key to staying healthy and happy in the long run.

#### Passage B

Have you ever heard of "food pairing"（食物搭配）? It is the idea that certain foods taste especially good together. Some pairings are obvious — like bread and butter, or tomatoes and eggs. But some are more surprising.

Take chocolate and salt, for example. A little salt on dark chocolate can make the chocolate taste even sweeter and richer. That's because salt can suppress（抑制）bitterness（苦味）while making sweetness stand out more.

Another surprising pairing is watermelon and cheese. In some Mediterranean（地中海的）countries, people eat watermelon with feta cheese（菲达奶酪）. The sweet, juicy watermelon and the salty, creamy cheese create a perfect balance.

Then there's the classic Chinese pairing — soy milk（豆浆）and youtiao（油条）. The warm, slightly sweet soy milk goes perfectly with the crispy, salty youtiao. This combination is not just about taste — it's a breakfast tradition that goes back hundreds of years.

Food pairing shows us that food is not just about eating. It's about discovery, creativity, and sometimes, happy accidents. So next time you're in the kitchen, don't be afraid to try something new — you might discover your own perfect pairing!

### 七、书面表达（参考范文）

Dear Mike,

I'm happy to introduce my favourite Chinese dish — jiaozi!

Jiaozi are dumplings filled with meat and vegetables. When they are freshly cooked, they **smell** amazing, and the steam rising from the plate **looks** so inviting. They **taste** delicious, especially with a little vinegar and soy sauce. The filling is juicy and flavourful.

I love jiaozi not just because they taste great, but also because making them brings my family together. We sit around the table, wrap each jiaozi by hand, and share stories. It feels warm and happy — like home.

I hope you can try jiaozi someday!

Yours,
Li Hua
