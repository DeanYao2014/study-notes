## 一、单元分析

### 1.1 主题与课文内容

| 板块 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| **主题** | 旅行（Travel）——国内旅行体验 + 探险家徐霞客 |
| **阅读 A** | 外国游客在中国旅行，尝试四川火锅（from 不适 → 享受） |
| **阅读 B** | 徐霞客——中国明代旅行家/地理学家，梦想走遍中国山水 |
| **文化意识** | "When in China, do as the Chinese do"（入乡随俗） |
| **跨学科** | 地理——中国地形地貌（peak, waterfall, plateau, glacier） |

### 1.2 语法核心

| 语法点 | 结构 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| **平级比较（肯定）** | as + 原级 + as | Cooking was **as fun as** eating. |
| **平级比较（否定）** | not as/so + 原级 + as | He is **not as tall as** his brother. |
| **倍数表达** | 倍数 + as...as | This room is **twice as big as** that one. |

> 🔥 **核心考点**：as...as 中间永远用**形容词/副词原级**，不能用比较级！
> - ✅ as **good** as
> - ❌ as **better** as

| 词性 | 数量 | 代表词 |

| 名词（旅行/自然） | 12+ | airport, waterfall, postcard, peak, thunder... |
| 动词（动作/心理） | 8+ | expect, prefer, discuss, accept, achieve... |

## 三、核心短语 & 句型

### 必背短语

| # | 短语 | 中文 | 考点 |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | **look forward to + doing** | 期待做某事 | to 是介词！接 doing |
| 2 | **places of interest** | 名胜古迹 | = tourist attractions |
| 3 | **dream of doing** | 梦想做某事 | of 也是介词 |
| 4 | **achieve one's dream** | 实现梦想 | = make one's dream come true |
| 5 | **find out** | 找出；查明 | 强调"经过调查发现" |
| 6 | **get around** | 各处走动 | = travel around |
| 7 | **in general** | 通常；普遍 | = generally speaking |
| 8 | arrive **in** + 大地方 | 到达大城市/国家 | arrive in Beijing |
| 9 | arrive **at** + 小地方 | 到达小地方 | arrive at the airport |
| 10 | When in China, **do as the Chinese do** | 入乡随俗 | as = 像……一样 |

### 重点句型模板

| 句型 | 中文 | 仿写 |
|------|------|------|

## 四、易错点清单

| # | 易错点 | 正解 | 错例 |
|---|--------|------|------|
| 1 | as...as 中间用**原级** | as **beautiful** as | ❌ as more beautiful as |
| 2 | look forward to + **doing** | look forward to **meeting** you | ❌ look forward to meet you |
| 3 | dream of + **doing** | dream of **becoming** | ❌ dream of to become |
| 4 | arrive **in** 大 / arrive **at** 小 | arrive **in** Shanghai / arrive **at** the station | 混淆 |
| 5 | excited (人感到兴奋) vs exciting (物令人兴奋) | I'm **excited**. / The trip is **exciting**. | 混用 |
| 6 | reply **to** sb. | reply to me | ❌ reply me |
| 7 | prefer A **to** B | prefer tea **to** coffee | ❌ prefer tea than coffee |
| 8 | discuss **sth.** (及物，不加 about) | discuss the problem | ❌ discuss about the problem |

### 三、阅读理解（共 10 题，每题 2 分，满分 20 分）

#### Passage A

Xu Xiake was a famous Chinese traveller and geographer. He was born in 1587 in Jiangsu. From an early age, Xu dreamed of visiting all of China's famous mountains, rivers and seas. His mother always supported him.

At the age of 22, Xu started his first long journey. For over 30 years, he travelled to many places in China. At that time, there were no cars or planes, so he had to walk or ride horses. The trips were not as comfortable as they are today, but Xu never gave up.

Xu kept a diary during his travels. He wrote down what he saw and discovered. His diary became the famous book *Xu Xiake's Travels* (《徐霞客游记》). It is not as simple as just a travel diary — it also includes important geographical discoveries. For example, Xu was the first person to find out that the Jinsha River is the true source（源头）of the Yangtze River.

Today, Xu's birthday (May 19th) is celebrated as China Tourism Day (中国旅游日).

**Choose the best answer:**

26. When was Xu Xiake born?
A. In 1578 &emsp; B. In 1587 &emsp; C. In 1687 &emsp; D. In 1785

27. How old was Xu when he started his first long journey?
A. 20 &emsp; B. 22 &emsp; C. 30 &emsp; D. 35

28. What does the underlined word "supported" mean in Chinese?
A. 反对 &emsp; B. 忽视 &emsp; C. 支持 &emsp; D. 批评

29. Which discovery did Xu Xiake make?
A. The Great Wall &emsp; B. The real source of the Yangtze River &emsp; C. The Li River &emsp; D. Mount Qomolangma

A. June 1st &emsp; B. October 1st &emsp; C. May 19th &emsp; D. April 22nd

#### Passage B

Dear Jenny,

I'm having a wonderful time in China! I've been here for two weeks and every day has been an adventure（冒险）.

Last week, I was in Chengdu. I tried Sichuan hot pot for the first time. At first, I thought it was too spicy（辣的）— my face felt like it was burning! But after a while, surprisingly, I started to enjoy the taste. Now I think hot pot is as delicious as the best food in my hometown. My Chinese friend told me, "When in China, do as the Chinese do." Eating spicy food is an important part of the culture here.

This week, I'm in Xi'an. Yesterday I visited the Terracotta Warriors（兵马俑）. They are as amazing as the pictures in books — even more amazing actually! Each warrior has a different face. Can you imagine that these were made over 2,000 years ago? Seeing such an ancient wonder in real life is completely different from seeing photos.

Tomorrow I'm going to climb Mount Hua（华山）. I've heard it's one of the most dangerous hikes in the world, but the view from the peak must be incredible. I'm really looking forward to it!

How is your summer going? Write back and tell me everything!

Love,
Amy
