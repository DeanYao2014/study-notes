## Unit 1 — The Secrets of Happiness 幸福的秘密

### 语法：一般过去时（Simple Past Tense）

#### be动词过去式

| 人称 | 现在 | 过去 |
|------|------|------|
| I | am | was |
| he/she/it | is | was |
| we/you/they | are | were |

#### 实义动词过去式

```
肯定：主语 + 动词过去式
否定：主语 + didn't + 动词原形
疑问：Did + 主语 + 动词原形？
```

**时间标志词**：yesterday, last week/month/year, ...ago, in 2008, just now

#### 规则动词过去式拼写

| 规则 | 例子 |
|------|------|
| 一般 +ed | play→played, walk→walked |
| 以e结尾 +d | like→liked, live→lived |
| 辅音+y → ied | study→studied, carry→carried |
| 重读闭音节双写+ed | stop→stopped, plan→planned |

#### 本单元不规则动词

put→put, keep→kept, sweep→swept, come→came, blow→blew, fall→fell, forget→forgot, choose→chose

### 核心词汇

| 词性 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 名词 | happiness, description, experience, customer, smile, decision, choice |
| 动词 | choose-chose, decide, blow-blew, fall-fell, forget-forgot, touch, complete, excite |
| 形容词 | exciting（物）/ excited（人）, magical, poor, freezing, empty, positive, surprised, rich |
| 副词 | finally, actually |

### 核心短语

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| put down | 放下 |
| keep doing sth | 继续做某事 |
| be full of... | 充满…… |
| to one's surprise | 令某人惊讶的是 |
| in fact | 事实上 |
| the key to happiness | 幸福的关键 |
| fill...with... | 用……填满 |
| laugh at | 嘲笑 |
| try to do sth | 尽力做某事 |
| decide to do sth | 决定做某事 |
| sweep across | 横扫 |
| all night long | 整夜 |
| spring from | 源于，来源于 |

### 重点句型
- Poor in things, rich in love. — 于物贫瘠，于爱富足。
- To my surprise, the family were happy together.
- Keep love in your heart. A life without it is like a sunless garden. — Oscar Wilde
- Happiness springs from doing good and helping others. — Plato

### 易错点
- **exciting**（令人兴奋的-物）vs **excited**（感到兴奋的-人）
- 否定句/疑问句中**动词还原**：`Did you went?` ✗ → `Did you go?` ✓
- `to one's surprise` 中 one's 要变形：to my/his/her surprise

## Unit 2 — Go for It! 全力以赴

### 语法：不定代词

| 代词 | 含义 | 关键区别 |
|------|------|---------|
| both | 两者都 | 指两个人或物 |
| all | 全都 | 指三个及以上 |
| either | 两者任一 | 二选一 |
| neither | 两者都不 | 两者全否定 |
| none | 全无 | 三者及以上全否定 |

**考点**：
- both...and... 谓语用复数
- either...or... / neither...nor... 就近原则
- none 可指人/物，常跟 of；no one 只指人，不跟 of

### 核心词汇

| 词性 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 名词 | marathon, breath, stomachache, dream, record, member, moment, purpose, charity, goal, victory, courage, spirit, competition, achievement, medal, success |
| 动词 | cheer, cross, raise, encourage, fight-fought, score, lead-led, improve, create, sink-sank, attack, continue |
| 形容词 | confident, lifelong, possible, brave, international, proud, fit |

### 核心短语

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| hold one's breath | 屏住呼吸 |
| sink or swim | 自生自灭，成败全靠自己 |
| fall behind | 落后 |
| finishing line | 终点线 |
| never give up | 永不放弃 |
| rise to every challenge | 应对每一次挑战 |
| last but not least | 最后但同样重要的 |
| pay attention | 注意 |

### 重点句型
- All of them held their breath. Would he make it?
- However, the players never gave up. They took every chance to learn and improve.
- There are still many difficulties ahead. But they will rise to every challenge, thanks to their shining spirit.

### 易错点
- **both** 指两者 vs **all** 指三者及以上——选择高频
- either...or... / neither...nor... 连接主语时**就近原则**
- `stomachache` 拼写：stomach + ache

## Unit 3 — Food Matters 食物很重要

### 语法：连系动词（Linking Verbs）

| 类别 | 动词 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be动词 | am/is/are/was/were | The food is delicious. |
| 感官类 | taste, smell, feel, sound, look | It tastes good. / It smells nice. |
| 变化类 | become, get, turn, go, grow | The meat went bad. |
| 保持类 | keep, remain, stay | Keep calm. |

**考点**：连系动词后接**形容词**，不接副词！
- It tastes **good**. ✓（不用 well）
- It sounds **beautiful**. ✓（不用 beautifully）

### 核心词汇

| 词性 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 名词 | beef, carrot, mutton, pot, recipe, ingredient, pancake, porridge, menu, hamburger, medicine, pill, connection, dessert, dynasty, folk tale, emperor, stomach, symbol, bridge, salt, pepper, oil, pan, plate |
| 动词 | remain, prove, order, scramble, beat, add, stir, store, suffer, compare |
| 形容词 | delicious, certain, sweet, plain, similar, special, golden, modern, salty, fat |

### 核心短语

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| bring back memories | 唤起回忆 |
| see...as... | 把……视为…… |
| compare...to... | 把……比作…… |
| a symbol of | ……的象征 |

### 重点句型
- The taste and smell of a certain food can often bring back memories.
- Many people see ice cream as a Western dessert.
- People often compare roujiamo to hamburgers, a symbol of American food.
- **Food is a bridge between cultures.**

### 易错点
- 连系动词 + 形容词（不是副词）：tastes **delicious** ✓
- compare **to**（比作）vs compare **with**（比较）
- `see...as...` = `regard...as...`

## Unit 4 — The Art of Having Fun 享受生活的艺术

### 语法：祈使句

| 类型 | 肯定 | 否定 |
|------|------|------|
| Do型 | 动词原形 + 其他 | Don't + 动词原形 + 其他 |
| Be型 | Be + 形容词/名词 | Don't be + 形容词/名词 |
| Let型 | Let + 宾语 + 动词原形 | Don't let... / Let...not... |
| No型 | — | No + 动名词/名词 |

**考点**：祈使句 + and/or
- Study hard **and** you will succeed.（顺承→成功）
- Hurry up, **or** you'll be late.（否则→迟到）

### 核心词汇

| 词性 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 名词 | tool, list, novel, magazine, feeling, importance, detail, conclusion, instruction, discussion, monster, couch, screen, neck, tennis, housework |
| 动词 | imagine, invite, discover, explore, notice, jog, hike |
| 形容词 | dull, alone, bored, funny, youthful, able, open-minded, unforgettable, dangerous, social, sore, deaf, real, balanced, fresh |

### 核心短语

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| do away with | 消除 |
| lift sb's spirits | 使某人开心起来 |
| couch potato | 电视迷，沙发土豆 |
| take time out | 抽时间 |

### 重点句型
- All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy.
- Fun can do away with bad feelings and lift our spirits.
- We don't stop playing because we grow old; we grow old because we stop playing. — 萧伯纳
- No one is an island.

### 易错点
- `bored`（感到无聊的-人）vs `boring`（令人无聊的-物）
- 祈使句**无主语**，动词原形开头
- Don't 后面永远跟**动词原形**

## Unit 5 — Amazing Nature 神奇的大自然 ⭐重点 Unit

### 语法：形容词/副词比较级与最高级

#### 规则变化

| 类型 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
|------|--------|--------|
| 一般词 | +er | +est |
| 以e结尾 | +r | +st |
| 重读闭音节 | 双写+er | 双写+est |
| 辅音+y | 变y为i+er | 变y为i+est |
| 多音节词 | more + 原级 | most + 原级 |

#### 不规则变化（必背）

| 原级 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
|------|--------|--------|
| good/well | better | best |
| bad/badly | worse | worst |
| many/much | more | most |
| little | less | least |
| far | farther/further | farthest/furthest |

#### 核心句型

| 句型 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 比较 | A + 动词 + 比较级 + than + B | He is taller than me. |
| 越来越 | 比较级 + and + 比较级 | It's getting colder and colder. |
| 越……越 | the + 比较级, the + 比较级 | The more, the better. |
| 最高 | A + 动词 + the + 最高级 + in/of | He is the tallest in class. |
| 其中之一 | one of + the + 最高级 + 复数 | one of the highest mountains |

### 核心词汇

| 词性 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 名词 | plateau, cloud, lotus, sky, grassland, field, landscape, mount, tide, exhibition, lungfish, lung, scientist, behaviour, surface, level, snake, wood, glacier |
| 动词 | express, describe, develop, lie-lay, suggest, disappear |
| 形容词 | soft, calm, common, local, salty, national, likely, present |

### 核心短语

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| be famous for | 因……而闻名 |
| such as | 例如 |
| all around | 到处，四周 |
| take less water | 少取水 |

### 重点句型
- My hometown is on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau. It is famous for the world's highest mountain.
- The colour white meets your eyes all around.
- There are a hundred kinds of blue in the water.
- The Dead Sea could die!

### 易错点
- 比较级**双写**条件：重读闭音节（hot→hotter, big→bigger, thin→thinner）
- 最高级前一般加 **the**
- `less` + 原级（不是比较级）：less water ✓
- far 两个比较级：farther（距离）/ further（程度）

## Unit 6 — Hitting the Road 踏上旅途

### 语法：as...as 平级比较

| 句型 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| as + 原级 + as | 和……一样 | cooking was **as fun as** eating |
| not as/so + 原级 + as | 不如…… | He is not as tall as his brother. |

**考点**：as...as 中间永远用**原级**，不用比较级！
- as **good** as ✓ / as **better** as ✗

### 核心词汇

| 词性 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 名词 | airport, lemon, mask, postcard, waterfall, thunder, painting, roller coaster, ant, beauty, award, traffic, truth, peak, discovery |
| 动词 | burn, expect, reply, prefer, discuss, accept, achieve, climb |
| 形容词 | deep, normal, ancient, further, curious, cultural, main, comfortable, powerful, huge, unexplored, geographical |

### 核心短语

| 短语 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| look forward to + doing | 期待做某事 |
| in general | 通常，普遍地 |
| places of interest | 名胜 |
| dream of doing | 梦想做某事 |
| achieve one's dream | 实现梦想 |
| find out | 找出，查明 |
| get around | 各处走动 |
| when in China, do as the Chinese do | 入乡随俗 |

### 重点句型
- I just couldn't wait to try Sichuan hot pot!
- When in China, do as the Chinese do!
- Surprisingly, I started to enjoy the taste.
- I'm really looking forward to the rest of my trip around China!
- From an early age, Xu dreamed of visiting China's seas and mountains.

### 易错点
- `look forward to + doing`（to 是介词！不是不定式）
- as...as 中间用**原级**
- `arrive in + 大地方` / `arrive at + 小地方`
- `excited`（人）vs `exciting`（物）

## 提分策略

**七下英语提分优先级：**

| 优先级 | 要做的事 | 原因 |
|--------|---------|------|
| 1 | 背不规则动词表（上面40+核心） | 过去时所有题的基础，不背一定错 |
| 2 | 背每单元核心短语（固定搭配） | 完形填空+语法填空直接考 |
| 3 | 精读每单元课文（2-3遍） | 培养语感、积累句型 |
| 4 | 比较级/最高级专项练习 | U5核心考点，中考必考 |
| 5 | 适量刷题（单选+完形+阅读） | 检验掌握、训练速度 |

### 刷题策略
- **单选**（语法填空）：快速刷，错题回看知识点
- **完形填空**：每周2-3篇，"通读→定位→搭配→复检"
- **阅读理解**：每天1篇保持手感
- **书面表达**：背句型模板+仿写
